
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Snow Meowl projects aim to provide a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone.

We value contributions regardless of experience level, background, or technical expertise.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Discuss technical disagreements professionally.
- Respect other people's time and contributions.
- Accept reasonable criticism and corrections.
- Focus discussions on ideas, code, and project goals.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language.
- Deliberate intimidation or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Abusive behavior toward contributors or maintainers.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer through the relevant GitHub profile or repository.

Reports will be reviewed as reasonably as possible. Depending on the circumstances, actions may include a warning, removal of content, restrictions on participation, or blocking an account.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces directly associated with Snow Meowl projects.

## Contact

Contact: https://github.com/snow-meowl

The maintainer will review reports and determine appropriate action.
